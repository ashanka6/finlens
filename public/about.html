About FinLens

FinLens simplifies the financial analysis of companies. Financial documents tend to be dense and confusing to those who have no financial knowledge. With the use of Financial Modeling Prep API, this website gathers financial information of various companies, analyzes it, and simplifies the information for users.

We aim to support individual and retail investors, small business owners, entrepreneurs, journalists, and bloggers by making company financial health easier to understand.
